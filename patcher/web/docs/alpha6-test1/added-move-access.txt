These eight items replace their previous contents. Other TMs and HMs retain
their contents and compatibility. New-move machine compatibility follows the
actual Blaze Black2 Redux v1.4.1 move, including its repurposed machine slots.

Earlier duplicate gifts/ground copies now give a Heart Scale. The displaced
Celadon/Pewter shop entries are removed; Goldenrod's other stock is retained.

FREE MOVE TUTORS
All four new tutors stand near the right side of their Pokémon Center's lobby.
Azalea, after Bugsy — Echoed Voice, Incinerate.
Goldenrod, after Whitney — Low Sweep, Electroweb.
Olivine, after Jasmine — Dual Chop, Foul Play, After You, Wonder Room,
  Magic Room, Quash.

  Retaliate.

Only verified Redux machine/tutor learners qualify. These tutors cost no BP,
items or money. Their unlocked moves also appear in portable Move Manager.
Existing native tutors keep their original locations and conditions; the
portable Move Manager teaches their unlocked compatible moves for free.
Struggle Bug is available through level-up and the Move Reminder. Round is
implemented, but has no TM, tutor, or level-up source in this build.

MOVE REMINDER
The Cianwood aide also unlocks the portable reminder after Chuck, including
when you already own TM62. The original reminder unlock still works.
Move Manager offers eligible moves learned at or below the Pokémon's level,
including level1 entries. It does not grant future level-up moves.

They also appear in Move Manager. Compatibility uses the original TM learners.
Water Pulse / Shock Wave — after Falkner.
Silver Wind / Natural Gift — after Whitney.
Secret Power — after Morty.
Iron Tail — after Jasmine.
Avalanche — after clearing the Radio Tower.
Torment — after first Champion Lance.

Owning one of the eight changed TMs also preserves access to its former move,
even before the listed legacy unlock. Move Manager records this entitlement
when opened. Existing saves keep their equipped moves and PP; already-owned
TMs immediately teach their new contents. No new save format is required.
